## Executive Overview

**FSM Quality Center** is a professional quality-management solution developed to support the structured review of field-service activity, strengthen operational quality visibility, and help teams identify cases that require attention, follow-up, or further assessment.

The system was created around real operational quality requirements in environments where installation, maintenance, service continuity, and customer-impacting outcomes must be monitored consistently rather than reviewed through disconnected or purely manual methods.

Its purpose is to provide a more organized quality-control environment that helps convert recurring operational information into clearer review outcomes, management-ready visibility, and a stronger basis for continuous quality improvement.

FSM Quality Center is designed as a practical business solution rather than a theoretical software exercise. It reflects the need to combine operational understanding, quality governance, software engineering, and structured information handling within one professional working approach.

This repository is a **public professional showcase only**. It presents the project’s purpose, scope, business value, and professional relevance while intentionally excluding implementation details, internal logic, proprietary quality rules, sensitive operational data, and source code.

## Business Context

Operational quality management in field-service environments requires more than reviewing isolated records. Teams often need to maintain consistent quality standards across recurring activities, identify exceptions, understand where follow-up is needed, and provide management with a reliable picture of operational quality.

When quality review depends heavily on manual comparison, dispersed files, repeated checks, or individually prepared outputs, several challenges may appear over time:

- Inconsistent quality-review practices
- Greater dependence on manual checking
- Slower identification of cases requiring attention
- Fragmented visibility across operational activity
- Difficulty maintaining consistent review standards
- Increased effort in preparing quality-management information
- Reduced continuity between operational activity and quality follow-up

FSM Quality Center was developed to support this type of environment through a more structured, repeatable, and business-oriented quality-management approach.

The project sits at the intersection of:

- **Software engineering**
- **Operational quality management**
- **Field-service operations**
- **Performance visibility**
- **Process improvement**
- **Data organization**
- **Exception and review management**
- **Decision support**
